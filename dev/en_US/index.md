## Plugin Transfer

Regardless of the reason, anyone may find themselves in a situation where they need to stop developing a plugin. To ensure this does not negatively impact users, it is recommended that you start a thread on the forum to see if another developer is interested in continuing to maintain it.

Once you've found a buyer, all you have to do is contact the Jeedom team to request the transfer. There are two options:
- Submit a support request from the original developer’s Market account by selecting the **Technical Support -> Developer** section. This request is submitted from the **Profile** page on the Market by clicking the **Submit a Support Request** button (the button on the far right),
- or send an email to the developer-specific address or to *support[a].jeedom.com* using the email address registered on the original developer's Market account.
